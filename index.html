About Me

I'm Durrani Hakim, an Electrical Commissioning Engineer working on a hyperscale mission-critical data center project. I take responsibility to manage for the electrical contractor and temporary 1 MVA substation contractor – following up progress, reviewing technical documents, supporting commissioning activities, and helping drive systems toward testing and handover.

My background is in Power Systems, and I find genuine fascination in how electricity moves through our world – from massive grid infrastructures to mission-critical facilities. I enjoy understanding complex systems, tracking issues, and finding practical solutions that keep projects moving forward. Whether it's reviewing technical documents, checking manufacturer specs against project requirements, or troubleshooting on site, I believe the best way to learn is by staying engaged and curious about how things actually work.

This is me in Berlin (December 2024). I had the opportunity to do an internship in Karlsruhe, Germany — an incredible experience that allowed me to learn and grow in a global environment.

A memorable moment after our first successful electrolyzer run. Huge thanks to Prof. Dr.-Ing. Kettner and Mr. Beerlage for their guidance and support throughout the project.

I had the chance to complete a full overhaul and rewinding of an induction motor during my Electrical Machines class — hands-on work that deepened my understanding of machine fundamentals.
